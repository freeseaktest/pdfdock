PDFDock bundled fonts — SIL Open Font License 1.1
==================================================

The following font files ship inside this project (public/fonts/) and
are served from the same origin as the app — no third-party CDN is
contacted at runtime. They are embedded subset-licensed into exported
PDFs on the visitor's device.

Each font is (c) its respective authors and licensed under the SIL
Open Font License, Version 1.1. The full license text is reproduced
below and also available at https://openfontlicense.org.

Fonts included:
  - Inter                (inter-regular.ttf, 89 KB)
  - Roboto               (roboto-regular.ttf, 50 KB)
  - Open Sans            (opensans-regular.ttf, 37 KB)
  - Lato                 (lato-regular.ttf, 100 KB)
  - Montserrat           (montserrat-regular.ttf, 85 KB)
  - Oswald               (oswald-regular.ttf, 25 KB)
  - Playfair Display     (playfairdisplay-regular.ttf, 74 KB)
  - Caveat               (caveat-regular.ttf, 116 KB)

The fonts are subsets limited to Latin coverage (Basic Latin,
Latin-1, Latin Extended punctuation) instantiated at their regular
weight.
